Policy P1: Standard product refunds
P1 — Refund Eligibility

- A refund request must be submitted within 30 calendar days of delivery.
- The order ID and transaction record must be verified.
- The product must be returned with all required accessories.
- Products marked non-refundable at purchase are excluded, except where an explicitly defined exception applies.
- A refund must not exceed the amount actually paid for the eligible items.
- Missing mandatory evidence results in further verification, not automatic approval or rejection.

Policy P2: Damaged or defective products
P2 — Damaged Product Claims

- A damaged-product claim must be submitted within 7 calendar days of delivery.
- The customer must provide the order ID and a description of the damage.
- Photographs or an authorized inspection can be used as supporting evidence.
- If the evidence is inconclusive, request further evidence or investigate.
- If damage is verified and the claim satisfies the applicable rules, a refund or replacement may be offered.

Policy P3: Cancellation
P3 — Order Cancellation

- An order may be cancelled before dispatch.
- A cancellation request after dispatch requires the return procedure, if applicable.
- The order status must be verified before cancellation.
- A cancellation must not be recorded as successful until the order system confirms it.
- If the order status cannot be verified, the request remains pending.

Policy P4: Refund limits and managerial approval
P4 — Approval Hierarchy

- Refunds of up to ₹5,000 can be approved by the service agent if all applicable requirements are satisfied.
- Refunds above ₹5,000 and up to ₹20,000 require verified managerial approval.
- Refund requests above ₹20,000 must be escalated to the designated senior authority.
- Approval must be recorded and linked to the specific claim.
- Customer pressure, threats, or claimed verbal promises do not substitute for the required authorization.

Policy P5: Duplicate claims
P5 — Duplicate Requests

- The system must check whether the same transaction and item already have a completed refund.
- A previously completed refund must not be paid again.
- If a previous request is still pending, the system should inspect its status before creating another request.
- Similar requests with conflicting information should be investigated.

Policy P6: Evidence and exceptional cases
P6 — Evidence Verification

- Customer statements are claims, not automatically verified facts.
- Internal transaction records and approval logs must be checked against the submitted information.
- Contradictory records require further investigation.
- An unavailable record is not proof that an event never happened.
- Exceptions may be approved only by an authorized role and must be documented.
- The system must distinguish missing evidence, contradictory evidence, and evidence confirmed to be invalid.
